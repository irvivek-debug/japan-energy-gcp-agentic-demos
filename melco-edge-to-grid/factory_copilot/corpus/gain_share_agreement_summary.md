# Energy Services Agreement Summary (Auto-DR gain share and BESS-as-a-Service)

Agreement ES-ATS-2026 between the plant and its equipment vendor's energy-services unit, from 2026-01-01. Fictional summary for a concept demo.

## Section 1 Services
The vendor supplies, operates and maintains a 4 MW / 8 MWh battery on a managed basis (BESS-as-a-Service), and runs Auto-DR and peak management across the plant's flexible loads with zero upfront cost to the plant.

## Section 2 Fees
BESS-as-a-Service: fixed fee of 3,000,000 JPY per month covering the battery, its warranty, insurance and operation. Auto-DR gain share: 25 % of verified eligible savings each month (contract range 20-30 %).

## Section 3 Eligible savings
Eligible: demand charge avoided, energy cost avoided by time shifting, DR availability and energy payments net of DR penalties, and imbalance cost avoided. Not eligible: efficiency savings delivered through analytics subscriptions. If eligible savings in a month are negative, the gain share is zero for that month.

## Section 4 Verification
Savings are verified from receiving-point and sub-meter data, JEPX prices and aggregator settlements. Sub-meter faults are flagged and the affected line items are held until the meter is repaired or an estimate is agreed.
