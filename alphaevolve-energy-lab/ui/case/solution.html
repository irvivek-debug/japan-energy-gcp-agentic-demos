This is where the technology comes in. The price book and the bidding strategy are short programs. An evolutionary search that speaks the AlphaEvolve contract proposes edits to them, a trusted simulator scores each edit, rules and a held-out set decide what counts, and a person signs off.

What it replaces

· Today

Pricing analysts and traders edit spreadsheets and rule files by hand, and the review is a meeting with no record of what was read.

· With the lab

A model proposes a code edit, a locked-down sandbox runs it, the simulator scores it, the rules and the held-out set judge it, and a named person holds to confirm that they read it. Promotion stays closed until the run comes from the managed service.
