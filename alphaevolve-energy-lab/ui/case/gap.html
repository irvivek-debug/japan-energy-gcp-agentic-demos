The case for change · 2 · The gap

The gap is measured on customers and days the search never saw.

A rule that only looks good on the data it was tuned on is not a gain. Every candidate is scored on a training set, and the best is scored again on a held-out set it never saw. Only that second number counts.

Reading the figures from the lab.

The change

What it replaces

· Today

A new pricing or bidding rule is back-tested on the same history it was tuned on, and the back-test becomes the business case.

· With the lab

The business case is the held-out result and nothing else. A candidate that breaks a customer or market rule on the held-out set earns nothing, however well it scored in training.
