The case for change · 1 · The case

Last year's price book was written for a cheaper market.

Kanto Balance Group renews its commercial and industrial customers every spring and trades its position every day. Both jobs run on rules: a cost-plus price book and a rule-based bidding strategy. Those rules were fitted to a market that has since moved.

Reading the figures from the lab.

The change

What it replaces

· Today

The renewal book is set once a year as cost plus a fixed margin. The bidding rules buy the forecast load and cycle the battery on a fixed pattern. When the market moves in the middle of the year, both keep doing what they did last year until someone rewrites them by hand.

· With the lab

Many candidate books and bidding rules are written and scored against this balance group's own history and its stress scenarios. A candidate that prices a customer segment out, or leans on imbalance, is stopped before it is scored. The winner is judged again on customers and days it never saw, and a person reads it before anything changes.
